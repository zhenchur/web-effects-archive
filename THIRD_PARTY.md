# Dependencies and assets

Cinema Letters and Fresh Content use GSAP 3.15.0. GSAP remains subject to its own license: https://gsap.com/standard-license/ . Both downloadable demos include GSAP, with its bundled license notice retained.

Vite and TypeScript are build tools; their licenses remain with their respective packages.

The archive page uses Lenis 1.3.27 under the MIT license: https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis/blob/main/LICENSE . Lenis is not included in either standalone module or demo bundle.

The archive uses system fonts, with optional host-provided TT Neoris for Fresh Content. No commercial font files, imagery, logos or other project assets from the Euler website are included. Sample words are editable text used to demonstrate the animation.

## Film Cards

Film Cards also uses GSAP 3.15.0 under https://gsap.com/standard-license/ . Its standalone demo includes GSAP with its bundled license notice retained. It does not use Motion+ code or paid components; https://motion.dev/examples/react-gallery-film is a visual reference only.

The local sample photographs are by Meriç Dağlı, NIR HIMI, Martina Nette, Dylan Taylor, Hans Ott, Pascal Debrunner, and Martin Casagrand, under https://unsplash.com/license . Individual source URLs are listed in `effects/film-cards/ASSETS.md`, included in the download. The photos are cropped/resized WebP files; no remote images or tracking requests are needed at runtime.
