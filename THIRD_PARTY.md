# Dependencies and assets

Cinema Letters uses GSAP 3.15.0. GSAP remains subject to its own license: https://gsap.com/standard-license/ . The downloadable demo includes GSAP, with its bundled license notice retained.

Vite and TypeScript are build tools; their licenses remain with their respective packages.

The archive page uses Lenis 1.3.27 under the MIT license: https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis/blob/main/LICENSE . Lenis is not included in the standalone Cinema Letters module or demo bundle.

The archive uses system fonts. No commercial font files, imagery, logos or other project assets from the Euler website are included. Sample words are editable text used to demonstrate the animation.
